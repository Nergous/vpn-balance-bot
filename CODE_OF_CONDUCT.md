# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in VPN Balance Bot to be respectful, safe, and useful for
people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete technical problems.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption,
doxing, publication of private data, and sustained personal attacks are not
accepted.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports or personal information in detail. Use
a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If none is
available, open a minimal issue asking for private contact without naming people
or describing sensitive events.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions consider context,
severity, prior behavior, and community safety.

Platform abuse or immediate safety threats should also be reported through
GitHub's abuse-reporting and blocking tools.
